Primary sort: IQR / median % — (p75 − p25) / median, the spread of the middle half of samples. Robust to tail outliers, so a single GC-pause or scheduler-preemption hit doesn't dominate. CV% (σ / median) is shown as a secondary column for tail-spike visibility — those two metrics can disagree, e.g. a scenario with 1M samples and one bad sample reads as 8000 % CV but < 100 % IQR/median because 99 % of samples cluster tightly.

IQR / median thresholds: < 5 % calm, 5 – 20 % watch, > 20 % noisy. Click any row for a box-plot breakdown.
